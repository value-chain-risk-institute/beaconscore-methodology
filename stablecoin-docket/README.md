# Stablecoin Docket — Citation Index & Synthesis Methodology

Neutral synthesis of the complete posted public comment record on the FDIC's GENIUS Act stablecoin rulemakings:
- **RIN 3064-AG19** — Requirements and Standards for FDIC-Supervised Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuers (PPSIs) and Insured Depository Institutions
- **RIN 3064-AG20** — Approval Requirements for Issuance of Payment Stablecoins by Subsidiaries of FDIC-Supervised IDIs

Prepared by the Value Chain Risk Institute (VCRI) as a public service, in support of VCRI's comment on RIN 3064-AG29. VCRI is itself a commenter in this record (AG19 entry #16); our own letters are treated identically to all others.

## Files
- **`citation-index.json`** — all 85 posted comment letters and memoranda, each with its source URL on fdic.gov. Machine-readable; every characterization in the synthesis is traceable to a source document.
- **`synthesis-methodology.md`** — how the record was read (full-corpus, structured per-letter notes, quote-verified) and the fault-line / opportunity map.

## Method
Every letter was retrieved from fdic.gov, read in full, and characterized against a fixed topic matrix. Quotations are verified verbatim against source PDFs. The letters themselves are public on fdic.gov and are linked, not re-hosted.
