# The Record Nobody Read: FDIC stablecoin dockets AG19 + AG20 — synthesis v0.1

*Value Chain Risk Institute. Method: all 85 posted comment letters retrieved from fdic.gov, read in full, characterized against a fixed topic matrix; quotations verified verbatim against source PDFs. Neutral public-service synthesis. CC BY 4.0.*

## I. The five stories the record tells

### 1. The yield war is a three-sided fight, and the middle is nearly empty
§ 350.3(b)(4)'s rebuttable presumption (extending the yield ban to affiliates/related third parties) drew more fire than anything else.
- **Kill it** (crypto: Coinbase, PayPal, Blockchain Assoc, Paradigm, CCI, Consensys, DFK): exceeds statute (rejected Warren/Hickenlooper amendments; Loper Bright), due-process/burden-shift objections, CLARITY Act pending. Coinbase: FDIC must bear the burden of proof.
- **Broaden it** (banks: TBA, Capitol Federal, IBAT, Meredith/Merrimack/Walpole, ICBA, Intl Bancshares; advocates: Better Markets, NCRC): all compensation ecosystem-wide (rewards, cashback, tokens); economic substance over form.
- **Make it administrable** (the thin middle): VCRI's four-factor rebuttal test + wrapper-token expansion; Attion's Reg Q interpretive frame; Auditchain's disclosure-based enforcement instead of presumption. Note: **Consensys directly attacks VCRI's wrapper position** (wrapper regulation belongs to the wrapping-protocol operator). A live adversarial exchange.

### 2. Underneath, this is a war about deposits, wearing crypto clothes
Dueling economics: ICBA's econometric model ($3-5T market → $850B community-bank lending loss, "one in five dollars"); TBA/McKinsey (~15% of converted funds return as reserves; $14-28B TX outflows); NCRC/TBAC (up to $6.6T migration); Wheeler citing Fed economists ($2-7T) — versus CCI/Coinbase citing the CEA study ($2.1B, 0.02%). Same docket, three orders of magnitude apart. The pass-through-insurance fight (Fiserv's FAIC/Scalia torpedo, Wheeler, PayPal, Ripple FOR; ABA, ICBA, TBA AGAINST) is really a fight over which balance sheet holds America's transactional money.

### 3. Everyone against the process
Sequencing objections are near-universal: comment periods closed before the rules they depend on exist (OCC final rule, FDIC §4 prudential NPR, Fed hasn't proposed at all). ABA: "Section 13's coordination obligation is not satisfied by parallel rulemakings that acknowledge divergence and invite post-comment resolution." ICBA: APA logical-outgrowth defect. Joint trades: statutory ambiguity over whether ONE agency finalizing triggers the whole Act (four readings). CSBS attacks the OCC's §16(a) reading. This record is pre-loading litigation.

### 4. The capacity vacuum is IN the record, and events have since confirmed it
- Better Markets: demand a published **supervisory readiness assessment** (staffing, tech, data, coordination); "no PPSI approvals until that capacity is demonstrably in place"; FDIC OIG 20% workforce cut cited; deemed-approval = "a reckless design choice."
- Rutkowski: "severe attrition of staff and expertise at federal banking agencies creates the precise conditions for a stablecoin-triggered banking panic."
- Ravnitzky: traditional exams don't capture validation, smart-contract governance, tech-provider concentration.
- Twilight: 200 community banks with disparate blockchain stacks = 200 distinct complex technical exams; proposes "one-to-many" supervisory model.
- Since these letters: the FDIC/OCC policy exodus (07-22, direct knowledge). The commenters demanded proof of capacity that has since left the building. AG29 docket (open since June) shows ONE posted letter — intake itself is lagging.

### 5. The measurement consensus — four hostile camps, one ask
The most striking pattern: commenters who agree on nothing converge on wanting **objective, continuous, third-party-verifiable measurement**:
- **Crypto**: objective criteria narrowly anchored ("operational resilience, cybersecurity, custody" — Blockchain Assoc); on-chain verification as supervision (Chainalysis, Stellar: on-chain = "legally sufficient source of truth," transparent chains earn reduced exams).
- **Banks**: codified application factors incl. technology architecture/cyber/ops-resilience/third-party dependencies (ABA: "blockchain validators, oracle providers, and cloud infrastructure"); vendor-concentration as named risk factor (the NH mutuals, Walpole); and **Capitol Federal Savings Bank**, on third-party reliance: "require the PPSI to comply with TPRM requirements and **allow the bank to rely upon the PPSI doing so without additional duties to investigate or confirm compliance**."
- **Auditors/standards bodies**: AICPA/CAQ (name AT-C 205 attestation standards, AICPA stablecoin criteria); XBRL US + Auditchain (machine-readable everything; BNP/validator-concentration disclosure taxonomy); GLEIF + ISO SAG (LEI/vLEI machine-readable entity identity — the LEI is a standardized global ID number for a legal organization; the vLEI its cryptographically verifiable form); **Empty Set (third-party AI behavioral-safety credentialing per the CRI FS AI RMF — "credential the AI system, not each institution," explicitly the SOC 2 analogy)**.
- **Advocates**: third-party operational-readiness assessments, penetration testing, pilots, stress tests as application evidence (Better Markets); Ravnitzky's full supervisory checklist.
Nobody names an existing instrument that does this for PPSIs, because none exists. The record repeatedly describes a need for a named, open methodology producing a portable, third-party-verifiable posture grade with defined reliance mechanics — an instrument that does not yet exist for this entity class.

## II. Fault-line map (quick reference)
- Yield presumption: crypto kill / banks broaden / VCRI-Attion-Auditchain make-administrable.
- Pass-through insurance: Fiserv-Wheeler-PayPal-Ripple FOR / ABA-ICBA-TBA-IBAT AGAINST (FDIC's proposal sides with banks).
- 40% reserve concentration: BNY + Blockchain Assoc + Custodia kill/soften / Better Markets tighten to 10% / VCRI + Ripple add functional-affiliate dimension / TDC optional safe harbor.
- Tokenized deposits: banks (HSBC, IBAT, USBC) protect the tech-neutral boundary / Circle + PayPal want separation or parity / NCRC + ICBA want a pause / CSBS wants joint state-federal guidance.
- Redemption/circuit breakers: Interledger (sub-minute, breakers trap holders) vs VCRI Letter C (mandatory minting circuit breaker) vs Attion (no breaker, fair value governs).
- Seizure/lawful orders: Mona Armande (burn-and-reissue carve-out) vs Grieve/SDS (warrant-required, anti-Kill-Switch) vs DDCP (key blast-radius architecture argument).
- Timelines: bank trades + Better Markets pause/extend vs MSBA supports fixed clocks.
